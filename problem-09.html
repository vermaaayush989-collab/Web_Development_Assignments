Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must maintain proper discipline inside the college campus and should respect teachers, staff members and fellow students.

Students should keep the classrooms and campus clean and follow all instructions given by the college authorities.

Attendance

Students are expected to attend their classes regularly and maintain the required attendance according to college rules.

Attendance should be maintained throughout the semester. Students should inform the concerned teacher in case of a genuine absence.

Examination

Students must carry their valid college identity card during examinations.

Students must follow all examination instructions and should not use any unauthorized material during the examination. Read the instructions carefully before starting the exam.

Assignment Submission

Students must complete and submit their assignments within the given deadline.
Late submissions may not be accepted without proper permission.

Assignment submission deadline: As announced by the respective faculty member.

Discipline

Students must behave responsibly and maintain discipline during academic and extracurricular activities.

Any behaviour that disturbs other students or affects the academic environment should be avoided.

Important Notice

Students are responsible for following all college rules and regulations. Rules may be updated when required by the college administration.

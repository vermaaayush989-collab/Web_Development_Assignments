GLA University Greater Noida Hosts Tech Event

Students Take Part in a Technology-Focused Event

GLA University Greater Noida recently organised a technology event for its students. The event was held at the university campus in Greater Noida and students from different courses took part in it.

The event was organised to give students a chance to learn about new technologies and improve their practical knowledge. Students actively participated in the activities organised during the event.

The event took place at the GLA University Greater Noida campus in August 2026. Students attended different sessions and activities related to technology and computer science.

The main purpose of the event was to encourage students to learn beyond their regular classroom studies. It also helped students understand how technology can be used in real-life situations. The event was a useful experience for the participating students.

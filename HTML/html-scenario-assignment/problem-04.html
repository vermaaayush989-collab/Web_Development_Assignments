Agra Travel Guide

About Agra

Agra is a famous city in Uttar Pradesh and is known for its historical places. It is a popular destination for people who want to explore Indian history and architecture.

Attractions

The Taj Mahal is the most famous attraction in Agra. Other places to visit include Agra Fort and Mehtab Bagh.

Taj Mahal Official Website

Culture

Agra has a rich culture and history. The city is known for its traditional handicrafts, marble work and historical architecture.

Food

Agra is also known for its local food. Petha is one of the most popular sweets of the city. Visitors can also try different North Indian dishes.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is a good time to visit Agra because the weather is generally more comfortable for sightseeing.

Travel Tips

Travel tip: Try to visit popular monuments early in the day to avoid large crowds. Carry water and comfortable shoes because there can be a lot of walking.

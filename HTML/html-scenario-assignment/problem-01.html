GLA University, Greater Noida

About

GLA University Greater Noida is a university campus located in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida. It provides students with education and opportunities to develop their skills.

Programs

GLA University Greater Noida offers different programs for students. Some of the programs include B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, and B.Tech Electronics and Computer Engineering.

Campus Life

Campus life includes academic activities, laboratories, student activities and different events. Students can also take part in activities outside their regular classes.

Admission

Admissions are open for the 2026 batch.
Students can check the admission details on the official website.

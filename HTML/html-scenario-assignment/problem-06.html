TechFest 2026

College Technology Festival

TechFest 2026 is a technology festival organised for college students. The event will give students a chance to learn about technology and take part in different activities.

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a fictional college event where students can explore different areas of technology. The event will include activities related to coding, artificial intelligence, computers and new technologies.

Students are invited to participate and show their technical skills.

Highlights

The festival will have coding activities, technology demonstrations, project presentations and talks about new technologies.

Students can also interact with other participants and learn from their ideas and projects.

Date and Venue

Date: 15 September 2026
Time: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Venue: College Auditorium, Greater Noida

Registration closes on 10 September 2026.

Speakers

The event will have guest speakers from the technology industry. They will talk about career opportunities, programming and the importance of learning new skills.

Students will also get a chance to ask questions and interact with the speakers.

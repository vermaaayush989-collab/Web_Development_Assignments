New Technology Festival Held at University

Students Showcase Their Innovative Projects

A technology festival was organized at a university campus to encourage students to explore new ideas and develop innovative solutions. Students from different departments participated in the event and presented their projects.

The event was held on 23 August 2026 and attracted students, teachers and visitors. Several projects focused on Artificial Intelligence, software development and modern technology. More than 100 students participated in the technology festival.

The students explained their projects to visitors and answered questions about their work. The event provided an opportunity for students to improve their technical and communication skills. The festival also encouraged students to think creatively and work on real-world problems.

Editor's Note

Events like these can help students gain practical experience and understand how the concepts they learn in classrooms can be applied to real-world situations.
